================================================================================
ARCHITECTURE INTEGRATION
================================================================================

Input Brain Data Flow:
  Microphone (16kHz, mono, 16-bit)
        ↓
  [AudioCapture - sounddevice]
        ↓
  [InputBrain.handle_audio_frame()]
        ├─ Ring Buffer (3-second history)
        ├─ VAD (energy-based detection)
        ├─ STT (faster-whisper inference)
        └─ Events published to Event Bus
           ├─ VAD_START
           ├─ VAD_END
           ├─ TRANSCRIPTION_START
           └─ TRANSCRIPTION_COMPLETE
              ↓
           [Event Bus] → Other Brains (2-7)

Ready for Brain Integration:
  - Brain 2 (Reasoning) - LLM inference on transcribed text
  - Brain 3 (Planning) - Validation and action planning
  - Brain 4 (Execution) - Tool dispatch
  - Brain 5 (Sentiment) - Emotion detection
  - Brain 6 (Avatar) - 3D model control
  - Brain 7 (Output) - TTS generation
